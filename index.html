DH to URDF

Enter standard Denavit–Hartenberg parameters (θi, di, ai, αi) or modified DH parameters (ai−1, αi−1, θi, di). Angles are in radians; lengths are in metres.

|θᵢ|dᵢ|aᵢ|αᵢ|joint| = standard DH; |aᵢ₋₁|αᵢ₋₁|θᵢ|dᵢ|joint| = modified DH. ASCII headers such as theta_i and alpha_i also work. Use true/R for revolute, false/P for prismatic. Expressions support pi, arithmetic, and parentheses.

Open MyModelRobot, then select the downloaded file under “URDF and STL files” or paste the text into its URDF editor. Each row creates a movable joint and a fixed frame joint. The generated joint ranges (±π radians and ±1 metre) are editable starting values, not measured robot limits.
